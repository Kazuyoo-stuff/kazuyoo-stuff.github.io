What is Celestial Lab?

Celestial Lab is where Android optimization experiments, tweaks, and lightweight modules are born. Every project is built around three pillars: efficiency, stability, and control — independently developed to improve performance and user experience across various Android devices.

What I Build

Every project under Celestial Lab is an Android system-level experiment — kernel tweaks, rendering pipelines, RAM handling, thermal behavior, and UI smoothness — aimed at squeezing more out of a phone without touching the stock experience it was built on. Modules range from small single-purpose tweaks to full WebUI tools, all developed and maintained solo, and shared for free with anyone running a rooted device.
